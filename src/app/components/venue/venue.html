Venue

Gulf University & Bahrain Polytechnic, Kingdom of Bahrain

ICSDI 2026 takes place at Gulf University and Bahrain Polytechnic in the Kingdom of Bahrain, in hybrid mode — with full virtual access for participants worldwide.

A Gateway to the Gulf

Bahrain sits at the heart of the Gulf region, well connected to the wider GCC and known for its heritage sites, beaches and vibrant conference scene. Gulf University and Bahrain Polytechnic offer a comfortable setting for two days of research, dialogue and collaboration.
